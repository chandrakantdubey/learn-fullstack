# Grafana

**Role:** Primary | **Layer:** Observability

## Mental model
Grafana is a visualization and alerting layer over metrics, logs, traces and other data sources.

## Learn
- dashboards and variables
- PromQL and log queries
- alert rules and notification policies
- annotations
- data-source configuration
- dashboard-as-code concepts

## Production
Build dashboards around user impact and SLOs, avoid dozens of vanity panels, control access, version important dashboards, and keep alert ownership explicit.

## Related
Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, Loki, Tempo, Jaeger.
